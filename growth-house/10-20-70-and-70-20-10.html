What Do 10-20-70 and 70-20-10 Have in Common?

29 September 2026

What do 10-20-70 and 70-20-10 have in common? And what happens when you combine them?

Both reveal something important about why change takes time.

IT structure, technology, AI, people and learning all need to come together to make business work and move towards goals. In theory this is pretty straight forward. In reality, it's a bit more complex than that.

A person within a dev team once shared his view on timelines with me:

"Think about pi, that's how any project will work. Things usually take about three times longer than expected."

I believe he was right, looking at how people actually adopt new ways of working.

How come organizations budget for technology, licenses and training but rarely the time it takes for people to change how they work?

This made me think about Boston Consulting Group's 10-20-70 model.

  • 10% - Algorithms & modelsThe actual AI technology & the "tool"
  • 20% - Data, infrastructure & integrationEverything needed to make the tool work: data quality, pipelines, architecture and systems.
  • 70% - People, processes & behaviourThe organizational transformation: new workflows, new decisions, new habits, new ways of working, culture, incentives and mindset.

Now, let's add the Learning model side by side: 70-20-10. The 70-20-10 learning model breaks down how people actually learn and change their behaviour:

  • 70% - Experiential learningLearning by doing: everyday practice, real tasks, trial-and-error, integrating new ways of working into daily routines.
  • 20% - Social learningLearning from others: colleagues, peers, managers, coaching, shadowing, feedback and shared workflows.
  • 10% - Formal learningCourses, training sessions, documentation, e-learning and certifications.

Something interesting happens when you place these two models side by side. The 70 per cent in both models have one thing in common: People, processes and behaviours. This is where organizations lose time, because this part depends on people, their motivation, habits, routines and relationships.

So, next time you wonder why things take longer than expected, think about pi and the budget you allocated for the 70% of the total. Did you budget and plan for it?
