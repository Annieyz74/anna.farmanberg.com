What If What We Focus On Reveals Who We Are?

10 September 2026

What if what we focus on reveals who we are?

I’ve been thinking a lot about how focus, values and behaviors are closely tied together. How the choices we make tell others who we are and what we stand for.

We live in a fast‑paced environment, making hundreds, maybe thousands of choices every day. Some require our full attention and engagement; others are automated and are shaped by habits and routines.

At work, someone might say they’re focusing on topic x right now. But their actions tend to reveal something different, what they truly value and what they actually want to focus on.

In private life, our choices often revolve around family, friends, health or the things that are closest to our hearts. We make choices even when we say we aren’t.

Time, pace, stress, opportunities, constraints and risk mitigation all influence our decisions. But we still have to live with the consequences.

Over the years, I’ve learned to focus on what I value and act as wisely as I can. Because my choices reflect who I am, what I value, and what I stand for. And sometimes we make the wrong call, that’s life. So, do I always get it right? Of course not, but I reflect, learn from it and move forward.

I’ve always liked Jesper Juul’s approach:

You can’t be consistent in every action; life simply doesn’t work that way. But you can stay true to your values and act accordingly.

I believe that approach is relevant in work, in relationships, in everyday decisions and in all aspects of life.

For me, that’s really what this is about: being honest with yourself, keeping in mind that focus, behavior and values are closely connected. We make lots of choices every day and those choices reveal what truly matters to us.

Acta non verba.
