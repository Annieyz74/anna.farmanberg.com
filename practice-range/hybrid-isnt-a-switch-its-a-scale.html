Hybrid Isn’t a Switch — It’s a Scale

3 September 2026

What if hybrid meetings could be more than logistics and actually become a way of working?

Hybrid meetings work, but only when they’re led and facilitated with intention. I’ve just completed Advantum Kompetens’ facilitation course, a practical training in designing workshops and facilitating them with intention: knowing when to lead and when to facilitate.

My biggest takeaway was understanding hybrid in a completely new way.

Hybrid as a scale, a dynamic movement between meeting mode and workshop mode.

You can design and shift between them, even in a 40–60-minute session and still serve the goal and purpose of the session.

Hybrid usually starts in meeting mode:

  • Present a clear goal, purpose, agenda and ground rules
  • Co-create ground rules to increase engagement (optional)

Then you move into facilitation and workshop mode when needed:

  • Short discussions
  • Quick exercises
  • Breakout rooms in triads or quartets
  • Focused reflection or conversations

Finally, you shift back into meeting mode to land the session:

  • Align
  • Assign action points (when relevant)
  • Define next steps
  • Close

Hybrid sessions require thorough preparation because the process design must be around the goal and purpose, this is key for success.

  • If you want to make a decision → design for direction, alignment and commitment.
  • If you want to provide recommendations or insights to those who will make the decision → design for exploration, reflection and shared understanding.

Remember that a hybrid session is a structured way of choosing when to lead and when to facilitate, based on what the session is meant to achieve. (There’s more to it than just adding a few exercises.)

I believe this flexible format fits today’s fast pace very well.

My reflections after this course is that it reinforced my strong belief in the power of meeting people where they are, inclusion, and creating a space where people feel comfortable saying what they really think.

We had two amazing days with Sofia Arvegård Wallin, who led by example, sharing her knowledge and experience with warmth and humor throughout the course. Thank you!

Also a warm thank you to Advantum Kompetens AB — everything worked very smoothly from start to finish. The whole experience made it easy to focus fully on learning and practice.
